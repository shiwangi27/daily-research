How a mind learns to move — one week at a time. A tiny 2-D baby teaches itself each motor milestone from scratch with REINFORCE. Scrub the weeks, tap a milestone, or press play to watch the whole first year unfold.

Week 6 Lifts head ★★★★ Mastered
Week 6 · head control
The first year, milestone by milestone →

Every pose is a real training run: a Gaussian policy over the baby's joint angles, learned by policy-gradient RL on a shaped reward (head height, hand-to-toy distance, balance, foot contact). Between milestones the body is interpolated. Offline & reproducible — python3 -m babysim.sim then python3 -m babysim.render.
